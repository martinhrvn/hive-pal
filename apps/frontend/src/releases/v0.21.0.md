# Version v0.21.0 <Name>

**Released:** Unreleased

## 🚀 What's New

- **Photos and files load from hivepal.app** — photos, documents and recordings now come from hivepal.app itself instead of the storage provider, and stay cached for hours, so pages you've already seen show their pictures right away (PR #326)
- **Link previews** — sharing a hivepal.app link in chat apps and social media now shows a preview image (PR #327)

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Faster repeat visits** — images, logos, icons and fonts on hivepal.app are now cached for a day instead of being re-checked on every page load (PR #325)
- **No more blank page after an update** — a script that was briefly missing during a deploy could be cached by the CDN and leave the app blank; those errors are no longer cached (PR #328)

## 🛠️ Self-Hosting

- **Files are served through the app** — browsers now load photos, documents and recordings from `/api/storage/files/...` on your own domain, with signed URLs (`JWT_SECRET`, falling back to `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`). An S3 bucket no longer needs public access or CORS for browsers. If a CDN in front of the app skips caching for `/api/*`, exempt `/api/storage/files/*` to cache media (PR #326)
- **Regenerate the link preview image** with `pnpm og:image` in `apps/frontend` (inside devenv); the template lives in `apps/frontend/og-image/` (PR #327)
